# Core Advising Rules

These rules are always injected into planning requests and are also backed by validation code.

## Source and Trust Rules

- Use only the provided knowledge files and current schedule file for course-planning facts.
- Do not invent course codes, prerequisites, term offerings, rotations, substitutions, or senior-only rules.
- Use official spaced course-code formatting, such as `ECO 113`.
- If a rule is missing or unclear, make a cautious plan and flag advisor confirmation.

## Completed and Current Courses

- Do not recommend a course the student has already completed.
- Do not recommend a course the student is currently taking unless the student clearly asks for a revised current-term plan.
- Treat completed courses and their prerequisites as satisfied.
- Treat current courses as satisfying prerequisites only for later terms.

## Requirement Groups

- Cognate requirement: `MAT 113 / MAT 121`.
- Statistics option: `MAT 217 / ECO 257 / PSY 217`.
- Money/finance option: `ECO 321 / BUSN 323`.
- Four additional ECO electives are required.
- Only one of the four ECO electives may be at the 100 level.
- Any ECO course may count as an ECO elective except required ECO courses: `ECO 113`, `ECO 114`, `ECO 213`, `ECO 214`, `ECO 257`, `ECO 321`, `ECO 461`.
- `BUSN 323`, `MAT 217`, and `PSY 217` are not ECO electives.
- If `MAT 217` or `PSY 217` satisfies the statistics option, do not count `ECO 257` as an elective by default; flag advisor confirmation if the student wants to use it that way.
- Because `ECO 257` has not been offered reliably, do not suggest it by itself. Use the group `MAT 217 / ECO 257 / PSY 217`.

## Timing and Pacing

- `ECO 461` requires senior standing and may only be scheduled in senior Winter.
- `ECO 461` requires `ECO 213`, `ECO 214`, and `ECO 257`, `MAT 217`, or `PSY 217` before the term it is taken.
- The statistics option must be completed no later than Fall of senior year because it is needed before Winter senior-year `ECO 461`.
- It is recommended to complete the statistics option by the end of junior year.
- It is recommended, when feasible, to complete both `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` by the end of junior year.
- Do not state that `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` must be completed by the end of junior year.
- Schedule `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` as soon as feasible after `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` and the appropriate introductory courses are complete.
- For freshman plans, schedule `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` no later than the end of sophomore year.
- The department has allowed concurrent enrollment in `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` with `ECO 213` or `ECO 214`, but this planner should avoid that when possible and should normally place math first.
- If `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` is current/in progress and `ECO 213` cannot be placed in its correct known 2026/2027 Fall term, do not move `ECO 213` to Winter 2026/2027. Wait and use future-year alternative wording instead.
- For incoming first-year students, default to at most two ECO courses in the first year: one in Fall and one in Winter.
- Do not schedule an incoming first-year student for a May Term ECO course in the first year. If known May options exist, mention them below the first-year block as options.
- For freshman and sophomore standard plans, do not accelerate the economics major to finish before senior year unless the student explicitly asks for an accelerated or early-graduation plan.
- For freshman and sophomore standard plans, aim for 2-3 ECO courses per full academic year when feasible.
- Do not schedule or mention `ECO 471` in standard plans. Include `ECO 471` only if the student explicitly asks for Senior Thesis.
- `ECO 471` requires senior standing and may only be scheduled in senior year.

## Load Rules

- Fall and Winter normally allow four total courses, but this planner shows only ECO/math-related courses.
- Do not schedule more than two economics courses in any Fall or Winter term.
- Do not schedule more than one economics course in May.
- Do not schedule more than one ECO elective in any term unless the student explicitly asks for a heavier economics load.
- For senior year, plan no more than one ECO elective unless absolutely necessary for major completion.

## Term Offering Rules

- Use the 2026/2027 schedule file as mandatory for 2026/2027.
- For 2026/2027, do not suggest any ECO course in a term where it is not listed in the schedule file.
- For years after 2026/2027, use cautious general rules when no schedule is available.
- For years after 2026/2027, write `ECO elective` instead of naming specific ECO electives unless the student provides updated offering information.
- `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` are Fall/Winter only, are not offered in May, and are not offered in the same term.
- For future unscheduled years, never display only `ECO 213` or only `ECO 214`. Write `ECO 213 or ECO 214` and later `ECO 214 or ECO 213` when exact term placement is unknown.
- Add a brief note below any year using those alternatives: `ECO 213 and ECO 214 are offered only in Fall or Winter, once per year each, so the exact future term is not known yet.`
- `MAT 113`, `MAT 121`, `MAT 217`, `PSY 217`, and `BUSN 323` are treated as Fall/Winter only, not May.

## Complete Plan Rules

- A returned plan must show every remaining major requirement, including four ECO electives, the math cognate, statistics option, money/finance option, `ECO 213`, `ECO 214`, and `ECO 461`.
- Fewer than four completed or planned ECO electives is invalid for a complete major plan.
- If the remaining requirements cannot fit under these rules, make the safest valid partial plan and explain the constraint briefly in notes; do not invent offerings or place courses in invalid terms.

## Advisor-Confirmation Cases

Flag advisor confirmation for:

- Transfer, AP, waiver, substitution, or older catalog-year cases.
- Permission courses, internships, directed studies, or uncertain course titles.
- Any case where a student wants `ECO 257` to count as an elective after satisfying statistics with `MAT 217` or `PSY 217`.
