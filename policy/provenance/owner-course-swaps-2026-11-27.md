# Owner decision: students may swap the Finance, Statistics and math choices — November 27, 2026

Authority: project owner and Economics Department domain expert, in the clean-up session dated 2026-11-27, after a live-app test found that only electives could be swapped. Owner's words: a student should be able to replace the Finance choice and the Statistics choice, as electives can be replaced. Owner choices on the two open questions: swaps may go into the same terms the planner itself uses when it builds plans ("Same terms the planner uses"), and the math choice is included ("Include the math choice too"). Owner instruction on ECO 461: statistics in the same term as ECO 461 must be blocked, and "move ECO 461 later" must not be suggested unless the plan has a later term for it (a fifth year). Effective from 2026-11-27 with no end date.

| User problem | Change |
|---|---|
| A student who prefers BUSN 323 to ECO 321 (or the reverse), or another statistics or math course, cannot change it; the only workaround misstated their history | "Take a different course" is offered for the requirements with a choice of courses: Finance, Statistics and the math choice. The choices come from the current (2025–26) requirement record, which every plan uses; the student's own catalog decides only whether the petition note appears |
| — | Swaps go only into terms the planner may use: published terms, plus future terms projected from past schedules under the existing rules (for example, no ECO 321 in an unpublished term). Every swap passes the full plan check, as every move does |
| A swap could have offered a stand-in such as "Statistics requirement" in place of a real course | Stand-ins are never offered as swaps |
| A blocked swap named the course being replaced ("MAT 217 must come before ECO 461" when swapping in ECO 257) | The reason names the course being swapped in |
| Every blocked move or swap that would leave a later course without its prerequisite said "Move {later course} later first", even when the plan has no later term for it (ECO 461 in a four-year or One Year Early plan) | That advice is given only when the plan has a later term for that course (for ECO 461: a later Winter with senior standing). Otherwise: "{course} must come before {later course}. Choose an earlier term." (new message `move.dependentPrerequisiteEarlier`) |
| The screen said "This is the last Winter before you graduate." next to every ECO 461, which is false in a fifth-year plan that places ECO 461 a year early | That sentence appears only when the plan has no later Winter |

Settled by the rule files, not by a new decision: statistics in ECO 461's term is blocked because the only same-term exception is ECO 213/214 (`CONCURRENT-INTERMEDIATE-ECO-461`); the petition note follows the catalog records, so a swap to a course the student's catalog does not list (BUSN 323 on the 2023–24 or 2024–25 catalog, PSY 217 on the 2023–24 catalog) brings the petition note, and swapping back removes it.
