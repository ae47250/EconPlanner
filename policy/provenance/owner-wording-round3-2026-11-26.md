Adjustments while applying:
- Item 9: in the code an “approved overload” means the advisor has already approved it (`lib/policy/term-load.js`). The owner’s sentence “This term also needs advisor approval for an overload” is therefore used for the term that is above the normal load without that approval, and the already-approved case says “This term uses an approved overload.”
- Item 12: removing the checklist note everywhere would have left plans with other statuses (for example verified or not yet feasible) with no schedule warning at all, so it is kept for those.
- Item 11 keeps the rule (the usual schedule assumes one Economics elective per unpublished Fall or Winter term; an extra one is possible but unconfirmed). The detail “course, funding, and instructor” is folded into “isn’t confirmed”.
- Item 13 (check only): the advisor-voice notes (“Confirm the student will be eligible…”, “Confirm whether the student’s AP credit…”) appear only in the PDF’s “For your advisor” section (`lib/reports/economics-html.js`), never on the student’s screen. No change.
- Item 5 (no-plan sentence) is not in this change: the same line also appears when the real cause is room saved for other courses, where “choose a later graduation year” is the wrong advice. Pending the owner’s choice.
- Item 4 (show the AP/transfer label once) is not in this change: the student panel’s duplicate check (`scripts/panel/lint.mjs`) recognises only the old credit sentence, and that check may not be edited without the owner’s approval.
