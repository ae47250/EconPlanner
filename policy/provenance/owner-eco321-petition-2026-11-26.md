# Owner decision: confirmed ECO 321 before yearly fit only to avoid a petition — November 26, 2026

Authority: project owner and Economics Department domain expert, explicit instruction in the clean-up session dated 2026-11-26. Effective from 2026-11-26 with no end date. Scope: nonacademic ranking of otherwise valid Economics plans. It does not change prerequisites, capacity, term limits, capstone rules, Finance choices, or schedule evidence.

Owner's words: the planner should prefer ECO 321 when the schedule confirms it; budget may keep the department from offering ECO 321 every year, but ECO 321 is preferable to BUSN 323. A petition is needed when ECO 321 fills the Finance slot and the student has also taken BUSN 323, to count BUSN 323 as one of the four electives.

Answers to the two follow-up questions:

1. "ECO 321 wins only to avoid a petition." For Balanced and Explore, a plan with ECO 321 in a confirmed (published) term comes before a plan with better yearly fit only when the other plan's Finance course would make the student file a catalog petition (a student on a catalog older than 2025–26, where BUSN 323 was not yet a Finance choice). Otherwise yearly fit still comes first, as in `plan-ranking-decision-2026-11-23.md`.
2. "Yes, BUSN 323 with the petition note." When ECO 321 is not confirmed in any term the student can use, the plan uses BUSN 323 and keeps the petition note.

Supersedes, in part: `plan-ranking-decision-2026-11-23.md`, the paragraph that puts yearly fit before a verified ECO 321 in every case. That paragraph still holds when no petition is involved.

How it works: the plan search keeps plans that miss the yearly spread only as a fallback. When every on-target plan would need the petition and the search also found a plan with a confirmed ECO 321, both go to the ranking, and ECO 321 wins.

Example: a sophomore on the 2024–25 catalog with ECO 113 done, planning on 2026-08-15, now gets ECO 321 in Winter 2027 in the Balanced plan instead of BUSN 323 in Fall 2028 with a petition.
