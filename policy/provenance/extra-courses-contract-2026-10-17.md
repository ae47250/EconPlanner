# Optional extra Economics courses — October 17, 2026

## Authority and scope

The project owner requested adding one or more Economics courses beyond the courses needed for the major. On October 17, 2026, the owner explicitly confirmed “Yes, separate Extra courses” in response to this proposed behavior: extra courses are marked “Extra” in the plan and PDF, count toward term load, follow all course checks, and do not replace a required course.

This is a submitted-plan software contract, not a change to academic requirements or an assertion of course availability.

## Contract

An extra placement is explicitly marked `extra: true` and has its own unique `extra-economics-N` placement identity in the existing requirementId field. That identity is not a degree requirement. Extra courses do not satisfy or replace required slots. Missing required slots remain missing. Removing an Extra course is allowed only if the remaining plan still passes validation, including the prerequisites of other courses.

Extra courses count toward course load, time conflicts, duplicate-course checks, prerequisite timing, and applicable offering limits. They follow the same exact-offering and projected-offering boundaries as other courses. A submitted Extra flag cannot waive any of those checks or turn a required placement into completed credit.

Published exact schedules govern named choices only within their documented scope. Historical observations may be displayed as past scheduling evidence, never as confirmed future offerings. Existing restrictions on named future electives remain in force: an unpublished future elective is a generic 200+ Economics elective, with the exact course to be chosen later. Showing historical choices does not override this restriction. May history is specific to May; Fall or Winter observations do not establish May availability.

Screen, saved plan, and PDF must retain the Extra distinction and the same validated placements. Student history, academic requirements, offering rules, and prerequisite rules are unchanged.
