# Wording review decisions — November 22, 2026

Authority: project owner, explicit decisions and wording-review instructions on 2026-11-22. Boundary: Economics student-entered history review, planning preferences, no-plan explanation, Hanover Core display, and student-facing wording. These decisions do not activate a new catalog, add course credit, change academic requirements, or extend future prerequisite exceptions.

## Owner decisions

### OD1 — Review in-progress courses with missing prerequisites

Treat a student-entered course marked “Taking now” with an unmet prerequisite like completed or transfer history with missing prerequisite evidence. Preserve the course, allow plan generation, and flag advisor review. Apply this only to student-entered history. Future courses placed by the planner must still satisfy prerequisite rules and term timing.

### OD2 — Hanover Core catalog applicability

Hanover Core rules for the 2023–24 and 2024–25 catalogs are the same as the 2025–26 rules. Remove the unconfirmed-applicability warning for those catalogs and show the same student-facing HS and W2 notes.

### OD3 — Finance planning preference

When no finance course is completed or in progress, prefer ECO 321 only in a term where it appears on a published, verified schedule and a valid plan using it meets the graduation target. ECO 321 is offered irregularly and depends on budget decisions. BUSN 323 is offered at least every year. Never schedule ECO 321 in an unpublished future term. Otherwise plan BUSN 323. This does not change finance requirement options, completed-course allocation, or the October 31, 2026 completed-pair rule. Keep the petition note only when a student on the 2023–24 or 2024–25 catalog has a plan using BUSN 323.

### OD4 — Course and term capacity explanation

For the “major courses left and terms” explanation, count capacity as four courses in Fall, four in Winter, and one in May, minus room reserved for other courses. A study-abroad term has zero capacity. Use the conditional overload ceiling only for terms with recorded advisor approval and confirmation that the student is not on academic probation. Show the explanation only when remaining required major courses exceed this total. Otherwise retain the specific reason.

### Allocation wording

Use the existing requirement-allocation rules to explain courses that do not count. Do not create or infer a new allocation rule.
