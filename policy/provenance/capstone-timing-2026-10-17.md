# Capstone timing clarification — October 17, 2026

Authority: project owner and Economics domain expert, direct clarification during the course-menu task.

The owner clarified that a student graduating in their fifth year who has senior standing may take ECO 461 in either the fourth or fifth year. The fifth year is probably preferable because the student may have completed more Economics courses by then. Accelerated students may reach the capstone in their third year under the existing accelerated-standing rules.

This is a display/advising preference, not a new eligibility restriction or solver ordering rule. Existing Winter, senior-standing, prerequisite, permission, and residency requirements remain authoritative. It does not require all students to take ECO 461 in their final year and does not block an otherwise valid earlier senior-standing Winter.

Student-facing menu presentation: “Senior capstone · Winter. Final year preferred.”
