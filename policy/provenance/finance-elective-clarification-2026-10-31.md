# Finance and elective clarification

## Authority and scope

Source: project-owner supplied email chain dated October 9–16, 2026, followed by the owner's explicit correction and implementation instructions on October 31, 2026. Student identity and personal details are omitted.

On October 13, the Registrar's representative explained that the faculty's 2024 approval allowed BUSN 323 as a substitute for required ECO 321, not general Economics elective credit. The courses are distinct, not cross-listed. Proposed catalog wording was a proposal, not an adopted amendment.

On October 16, the representative confirmed that a student who has taken both ECO 321 and BUSN 323 could petition to count BUSN 323 as an Economics elective with ECO 321 fulfilling finance. This confirms an opportunity to petition, not an approved individual petition.

## Owner-authorized implementation

Effective for plans generated or revalidated from October 31, 2026 under the existing activated Economics requirement set. Preserve the catalog-specific finance alternatives and all course prerequisites. This does not activate a newer catalog or retroactively amend historical catalog text.

Neither ECO 321 nor BUSN 323 receives automatic Economics elective credit, regardless of the other course's finance allocation. Both remain distinct recorded courses. When both have eligible completed evidence, prefer ECO 321 for finance. BUSN 323 remains unused for Economics requirements and the planner shows the petition notice. The planner has no petition approval-entry workflow and does not apply elective credit even when submitted data claims approval. A missing elective remains outstanding and must be planned normally.

The notice requires both courses to have verified, full-unit completed evidence. In-progress, planned, transfer, uncertain, failed, withdrawn, repeated, and provisional-source evidence cannot establish this completed-pair notice. A notice alone does not reduce plan readiness; an unsatisfied requirement retains its existing effect. The notice is separate from routine catalog-change SAC paperwork and is not described as a formality.

This explicitly supersedes the automatic BUSN 323 finance / ECO 321 elective pairing in the compatibility profile, catalog allocation record, and flexible-requirements differences. It does not alter course availability, cross-major credit rules, prerequisite equivalence, or other electives.
