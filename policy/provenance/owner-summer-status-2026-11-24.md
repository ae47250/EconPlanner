## Decision

Hanover's Summer Economics courses are online and run only if enough students enroll. A Summer placement is therefore never fully confirmed. Use the plugin value `enrollment_dependent` for a course whose source is `summer-last-resort`; classify a valid plan containing such a course as conditionally feasible.

Keep the schedule provenance and publication status derived from the existing published schedule evidence. Do not infer seat availability, schedule integrity, or a different ranking. This decision applies when Summer sections are published. The 2026–2027 schedule has no Summer sections (`policy/schedules/2026-2027.json`, coverage `UNAVAILABLE`), so it changes no live plan under that schedule.
