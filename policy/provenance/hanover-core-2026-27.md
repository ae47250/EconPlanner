# 2026–27 Hanover Core designations (informational)

Source: [Hanover College Academic Catalog 2026–2027](https://catalog.hanover.edu/), introduction and Hanover Core Curriculum; [Hanover Economics course descriptions](https://www.hanover.edu/academics/programs/economics/). Reviewed 2026-11-15 against the supplied 2026–27 catalog archive.

The catalog says the college-wide curriculum follows the academic year of first enrollment, while major requirements follow the declaration catalog. The Hanover Core lists two Historical and Social Perspectives (HS) Knowledge courses from different disciplines. The Economics descriptions identify ECO 113 and ECO 114 as partially satisfying HS Knowledge and ECO 461 as satisfying Writing 2 (W2) Skills. Both ECO 113 and ECO 114 are Economics courses and therefore cannot alone fulfill the two-discipline HS requirement.

Scope: 2026–27 first-enrollment college-wide curriculum information only. It does not change the activated 2025–26 Economics major requirements, verify individual completed credit, decide eligibility, or authorize inference for other entry years. Older CCR/ACE terminology remains on its supported 2025–26 boundary. Conflicting or unknown cohort records remain unresolved.
