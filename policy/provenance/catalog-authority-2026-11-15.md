## Authority

## MAJOR-AND-COURSE-SCOPE

The 2026–2027 Hanover College Academic Catalog is the source for the complete
Economics major requirement set and course rules for its published scope. The
Economics major set is governed by the catalog in effect when the student
formally declares Economics. College-wide Core rules continue to follow first
enrollment, as recorded separately in `hanover-core-2026-27.md`.

## Owner decisions

- Apply the complete requirement set for the student's declaration catalog
  year. Do not combine individual requirements across catalog years.
- An eligible older student may use a newer, more flexible complete set only
  when the required approval is documented. No approval is inferred from a
  comparison, a petition recommendation, or a missing declaration year.
- Apply a course's prerequisite rule for its enrollment term, independently of
  entry year and major declaration year. Preserve a documented older exception
  unless an authoritative newer rule expressly supersedes it.
- Mixed prerequisite changes require review. Do not union alternatives
  automatically. Preserve completed historical coursework.
- An unknown major declaration year leaves the major requirement selection
  preliminary and unconfirmed.

## Verified historical declaration-year sets

The official 2023–2024 Economics major lists ECO 257 or MAT 217 for statistics,
ECO 321 for money/finance, and a passing comprehensive evaluation. Its ECO 461
listing requires ECO 213, ECO 214, and ECO 257 or MAT 217. The official
2024–2025 major adds PSY 217 as a statistics option, retains ECO 321, and does
not list an Economics comprehensive evaluation. Its ECO 461 listing includes
PSY 217. These match the complete historical records for their documented
declaration years; the owner authorized activation after this verification.

The catalogs document Fall class starts on 2023-08-28, 2024-08-26, and
2026-08-24. The canonical academic calendar documents 2025-08-25. These are
instructional term dates, not claims that the catalogs became legally effective
on those days. The records identify an academic year and its first instructional
term rather than inventing an August 1 effective date.

Approval to switch an older declarant to a newer complete major set remains
unavailable until a defined approval record is implemented. No newer set is
inferred or assembled from individual favorable requirements.
