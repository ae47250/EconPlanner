# Economics Major Requirements and Prerequisites

## Use of This File

Before creating or revising any economics major course plan, consult this file.

This file is for course-planning support. It is not a replacement for the official college catalog or advisor confirmation. If a requirement, prerequisite, course substitution, permission rule, or offering pattern is uncertain, flag it for advisor confirmation.

## Core Scheduling Rule

Never place a course before any of its prerequisites.

A course may be scheduled only after all prerequisites have been completed in a prior term.

If a prerequisite is planned, it must appear in an earlier term, never in the same or a later term.

Do not assume that a course is offered in a specific term unless the user provides the current schedule or the planning instructions explicitly say to use a known offering pattern.

## Economics Major Requirements

The economics major requires:

- 11 major courses
- 1 cognate course
- Total: 12 courses

Required economics courses:

- ECO 113 — Principles of Microeconomics
- ECO 114 — Principles of Macroeconomics
- ECO 213 — Intermediate Microeconomics
- ECO 214 — Intermediate Macroeconomics
- ECO 257 — Quantitative Methods for Business/Economics, or MAT 217, or PSY 217
- ECO 321 — Money & Financial Markets, or BUSN 323
- ECO 461 — Senior Seminar: Econometrics

Cognate requirement:

- MAT 113 or MAT 121

Elective requirement:

- Four additional ECO courses
- Only one of the four additional ECO electives may be at the 100 level
- Do not count the cognate course as one of the 11 major courses
- Do not count a non-ECO substitute as an ECO elective unless advisor confirmation is provided

## Minor Requirements Reference

The economics minor requires:

- ECO 113
- ECO 114
- ECO 213 or ECO 214
- Any three additional ECO courses, excluding ECO 257 and MAT 217
- Total: 6 minor courses

Use this minor section only when the user asks about the economics minor.

## Required Course Sequence Logic

Use this sequence logic when building plans:

1. ECO 113 and ECO 114 are the normal starting courses.
2. MAT 113 or MAT 121 is needed before ECO 213 and ECO 214.
3. ECO 213 requires ECO 113 plus MAT 113 or MAT 121.
4. ECO 214 requires ECO 114 plus MAT 113 or MAT 121.
5. ECO 257 requires ECO 113 or ECO 114.
6. ECO 321 requires ECO 113 and ECO 114.
7. ECO 461 requires ECO 213, ECO 214, and ECO 257 or MAT 217.
8. ECO 471 requires ECO 213, ECO 214, and ECO 321.

Advisor-confirmation flags:

- If a student uses PSY 217 instead of ECO 257 or MAT 217, confirm whether it satisfies the prerequisite pathway into ECO 461.
- If a student uses BUSN 323 instead of ECO 321, confirm whether it satisfies any later course prerequisite that specifically names ECO 321.
- ECO 357 requires department permission.
- Directed study and special topics courses may require advisor or department approval.

## Elective Planning Rules

When the student needs an economics elective but no specific elective schedule is available, use the generic label “ECO elective” rather than naming a specific course.

Do not place more than one ECO elective in the same term unless the user explicitly asks for a heavier economics load.

If naming a specific elective, check its prerequisite first. Examples:

- Labor Economics is ECO 331 and requires ECO 213 first.
- Economics of the European Union is ECO 222 and requires ECO 114 first.
- International Trade & Finance is ECO 333 and requires ECO 114 first.
- Managerial Economics is ECO 335 and requires ECO 213, ECO 257, and MAT 121 or equivalent first.

Special Topics, Directed Study, Internship, and cross-listed courses require extra caution:

- ECO 160, ECO 260, and ECO 360 are Special Topics. The topic may change.
- ECO 307 and ECO 370 are Directed Study courses. Confirm whether they count and how many units they carry.
- ECO 357 is Internship and requires department permission.
- ECO 234 is cross-listed with PHI 234. Count either course code as the same course; advisor approval is not required.

## Avoid These Planning Errors

Do not call Labor Economics ECO 222, ECO 333, or a generic elective. Labor Economics is ECO 331.

Do not place ECO 213 before ECO 113 and MAT 113 or MAT 121.

Do not place ECO 214 before ECO 114 and MAT 113 or MAT 121.

Do not place ECO 461 before ECO 213, ECO 214, and ECO 257 or MAT 217.

Do not treat PSY 217 as automatically satisfying the ECO 461 prerequisite without advisor confirmation.

Do not treat BUSN 323 as automatically satisfying ECO 471 prerequisites without advisor confirmation.

Do not assume that catalog offering notes guarantee current-year availability.

Do not invent prerequisites for courses where no prerequisite is listed in the source material. Instead, say that no prerequisite is listed here and flag advisor/catalog confirmation if needed.
