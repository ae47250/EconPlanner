# Economics Department Confirmed Planning Policies

## Status and provenance

- Source type: department-confirmed local policy
- Confirmed by: Economics Department representative and project owner
- Confirmation date: 2026-08-01
- Use: EconPlanner course-generation and validation rules
- Authority boundary: These policies supplement the applicable Hanover catalog. They do not change College-wide degree or course-load rules.

## Confirmed policies

1. PSY 217 is a valid statistics prerequisite for ECO 461, alongside ECO 257 and MAT 217.
2. A compressed graduation plan may treat the student's final academic year as senior standing for ECO 461 only when the student reports that the Registrar or advisor confirmed senior standing for that exact academic year. Normal plans reach senior standing in the fourth college year. An extended five-year plan remains senior in both the fourth and fifth years, so ECO 461 should use the earliest otherwise-valid senior Winter.
3. MAT 113 or MAT 121 may be taken concurrently with ECO 213 or ECO 214 with approval from the ECO 213 or ECO 214 instructor. The applicable introductory Economics prerequisite must still be completed first.
4. When exactly one of ECO 213 or ECO 214 will be successfully completed before the target ECO 461 term, the other may be taken concurrently with ECO 461 with explicit Economics Department approval. Courses legitimately available before the target term include eligible completed courses, accepted transfer courses, a current in-progress course scheduled to finish before the target term, and a course placed in an earlier generated term. A course in the ECO 461 term or a later term does not count as completed before ECO 461, and uncertain or unaccepted history, equivalency, substitution, waiver, or prerequisite evidence does not count. When the earlier intermediate is current in-progress or planned in an earlier generated term, the plan remains conditional on its successful completion. The concurrent intermediate's own prerequisites must be satisfied, and the statistics prerequisite must be completed before ECO 461.
5. ECO 213 and ECO 214 are normally separated by the annual offering pattern: one is offered in Fall and the other in Winter. This is an offering pattern, not a prohibition against enrolling in both during one term if a future verified schedule were to offer both.
6. ECO 461 is offered only in Winter, requires senior standing, and must be completed at Hanover College as the Economics capstone.
7. No more than two Economics courses in Fall or Winter is a preferred advising load, not a hard academic limit. When additional Economics courses are necessary for graduation, the plan may include up to the normal four-course total term load and must display a heavy-major-load warning.
8. Every ECO 260 Special Topics course counts as one of the four Economics electives.
9. Plans must show whether a verified section is open, closed, waitlisted, or has unknown availability. Closed sections cannot support a feasible plan without an available alternative; waitlisted sections make a plan conditional.
10. The major requires four qualifying ECO electives. Inside the verified schedule horizon, use verified named courses and sections when available; a generic placeholder may not replace a known verified offering. Beyond that horizon, separate future requirement slots may each display as `ECO elective`, but each must retain a distinct internal identity based on its requirement slot or plan position. Placeholder slots count as planned requirements, not completed courses. Reusing the same actual course or the same placeholder slot is prohibited, while four distinct future elective slots may satisfy the four-elective planning requirement.

## Schedule evidence policy

Official schedule exports are preferred. Complete, legible schedule screenshots are acceptable when they show the academic year, term, course, section, title, instructor, credits, dates, meeting pattern, delivery/location information when available, seats, and section status. Screenshot-derived data must retain a verification timestamp and source-file provenance.
