# Owner-confirmed Economics catalog-flexibility policy

- Confirmation date: 2026-08-06
- Reviewer: Project owner and Economics Department domain expert
- Authority boundary: Economics-major requirements and documented Economics Department flexibility only
- Stable rule ID: `CATALOG-LATEST-FLEXIBLE-MAJOR-FALLBACK`

The owner confirmed in the current Codex task that a student may request newer, more flexible Economics-major requirements. When the declaration catalog is unknown, the latest authoritative published Economics catalog may be used for conditional planning, with all plausible older years preserved and advisor confirmation required. This does not prove that the student changed catalog years and does not alter College-wide graduation, residency, general-education, grade, or institution-wide rules.

Known flexibility changes are PSY 217 as a Statistics option, BUSN 323 as a Money/Finance option, and removal of the Economics comprehensive examination or evaluation when supported by the applicable catalog evidence. A newer stricter or materially conflicting rule may not be silently imposed, and favorable pieces from multiple catalogs may not be combined without authority.
