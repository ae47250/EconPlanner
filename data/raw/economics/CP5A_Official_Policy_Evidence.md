# CP5A official policy evidence

Retrieved and reviewed on 2026-08-08. This record is limited to the claims below; it does not create course sections or historical offering observations.

## College course-load policy

Source: Hanover College 2025-2026 Catalog, `https://catalog.hanover.edu/`.

- The normal course load is 4 course units in Fall, 4 course units in Winter, and 1 course unit in Spring Term.
- With advisor approval, a student who is not currently on academic probation may register for as many as 5.25 course units in Fall, 5.25 in Winter, and 1.25 in Spring Term.
- The higher values are conditional ceilings. They are not default planner capacity and may not be used unless both required student-specific facts are recorded.

## Relevant general-degree terminology

Source: Hanover College Academic Affairs, `https://academicaffairs.hanover.edu/development/curriculum.php`.

- Hanover describes Areas of Competency and Engagement as ACEs and Core Curriculum Requirements as CCRs.
- Historical and Social Perspectives (HS) requires two course units from different disciplines.
- W2 is the second writing-designated course within the Writing ACE.

## Economics course statements

Source: official Hanover Economics program pages, `https://catalog.hanover.edu/programs/economics.php` and `https://www.hanover.edu/academics/programs/economics/`.

- ECO 113, ECO 114, and ECO 161 are identified as courses that partially satisfy the HS requirement; an Economics course alone must never be presented as completing the full HS requirement.
- ECO 461 is identified as satisfying W2.
- ECO 222 is described as offered in Spring Term and requires ECO 114. This supports a named Spring/May planning projection for ECO 222 in an unpublished year, but it does not create an exact section.
- ECO 234 is identical to PHI 234 and must count once under the canonical cross-list rule.

## Supplied declaration form

Source: `data/raw/economics/ECON Major Declaration.doc`, visually reviewed as a one-page legacy Word declaration form.

- The form lists MAT 113 or MAT 121 as the Economics cognate.
- It identifies ECO 113 and ECO 114 with HS and ECO 461 with W2.
- The form's struck-through elective rows and accompanying near-future note are not historical offering observations and are not activated as ranking probabilities or recurrence evidence.

## Scope boundary

The student's applicable general-degree catalog year is separate from the department's owner-designated flexible Economics-major requirement set. If authoritative general-degree evidence for the student's applicable year is unavailable, CCR/ACE information must be omitted or marked unavailable and must not affect plan validity, readiness, or advisor-action status.
