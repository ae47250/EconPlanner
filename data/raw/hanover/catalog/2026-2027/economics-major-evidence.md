# Hanover College Catalog 2026–2027 — Economics evidence

Source: Hanover College Academic Catalog 2026–2027, https://catalog.hanover.edu/.
Retrieved 2026-11-15. The published online catalog is the primary source.
The catalog calendar lists Fall 2026 classes beginning 2026-08-24; it does not
state that this catalog took effect on 2026-08-01.

## Economics-Major

The catalog states that Economics majors complete ECO 113 and ECO 114 (highly
recommended by the end of sophomore year), ECO 213, ECO 214, one of ECO 257,
MAT 217, or PSY 217, one of ECO 321 or BUSN 323, ECO 461 as the culminating
experience, four other Economics courses (no more than one at the 100 level),
and one cognate course, MAT 113 or MAT 121. The catalog total is 11 major
courses plus one cognate.

## Economics-Courses

The catalog course listing says ECO 221 requires ECO 113, ECO 114, or ECO 161.
It lists ECO 461 prerequisites as ECO 213, ECO 214, and ECO 257 or MAT 217.
The department-confirmed ECO 461 pathway also permits PSY 217; retain that
documented department exception with its existing provenance.

This is the complete published major set for declarations governed by the
2026–2027 catalog. It does not approve applying a partial mix of this set to an
older declaration cohort.
