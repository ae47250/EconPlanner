## Hanover-Core-Curriculum

The catalog states that the Hanover Core curriculum and degree requirements are
the ones published for the academic year in which a student first enrolls.
Students new to Hanover beginning Fall 2026 follow the Hanover Core curriculum.
Historical and Social Perspectives requires two courses from different
disciplines. The Skills curriculum requires one W2 course.

## Economics-Courses

The Economics course descriptions say ECO 113 and ECO 114 each partially
satisfy HS Knowledge. ECO 461 satisfies the W2 Skills requirement. ECO 113 and
ECO 114 are both Economics courses, so they cannot alone fulfill the two-
discipline HS requirement.

This evidence supports informational report wording only. It does not verify
individual student credit or activate a new Economics major requirement set.
