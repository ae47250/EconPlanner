# Owner-approved planning-flexibility contract

Compiled policy: `econ-planning-flexibility.7` (`sha256:821f6dfbbb0f694196debbe93f0082a9b703eec2f2d5b23c57df94d11174850e`).

- Accelerated one-year-early plans assume senior standing in the final planning year; no student confirmation is requested and readiness is unchanged by the assumption alone.
- Published schedules remain authoritative. Later unpublished schedules use academically checked placements with `PLANNING_PROJECTION` provenance and one informational notice.
- Economics planning uses the latest verified, activated, owner-approved requirement set, currently 2025-2026. Catalog discovery never activates a new requirement set; incoming catalog years remain comparison and degree-audit metadata.
- Material incoming-catalog differences create a nonblocking SAC petition notification, not a substantive advisor action.
- A future owner-supplied Excel history may support informational offering likelihoods only when data coverage and method are disclosed. No likelihood data or probability is present in this release.
- CCR/ACE information uses the student's supported general-degree catalog separately from the Economics major requirement set and has no readiness or feasibility effect.
- Mathematics equivalencies require an explicit cognate, prerequisite, or both scope with complete approval evidence; no equivalencies are activated in this release.
- Balanced, Explore the Major, and One Year Early use the strategy IDs, public labels, eligibility boundaries, definitions, and nonacademic ranking preferences in the compiled strategy contract.
- Balanced places annual remainders in later usable years. Annual and within-year targets use actual capacity after fixed current courses and other reservations; preferred Spring/May load never exceeds one.
- Explore the Major remains limited to effective freshman and sophomore contexts, must be lighter than and materially distinct from its selected same-context Balanced baseline, and uses a soft nondecreasing backloaded target. Its preferred two-course first-year shape is 1/1/0, followed by the other separated shapes, with same-term placement retained only as a lower-ranked rescue.
- One Year Early remains limited to effective freshman, sophomore, and junior contexts; ineligible saved or submitted requests normalize to the standard graduation timeline without changing academic feasibility.
- No 2026-2027 catalog is compiled or activated, and the active Decision Engine mapping is unchanged.
