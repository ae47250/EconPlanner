## How the checks were done (evidence)

Three read-only scripts live outside the repository in `/tmp/review4scripts/`:

- **`check.py`** parses every snapshot's screen and PDF grid and compares it with `plans/<id>.json`. It also checks every checklist row's term against the grid, and checks every "Before taking X in T, finish …" line against the prerequisite records in `policy/rules/prerequisites.json`: wrong term, wrong order, missing or omitted prerequisites. It also checks:
  - every "This plan covers … through …" line
  - every "This plan has N Economics courses in T" line
  - every "This plan places BUSN 323 in T" line
  - every "We don't know if there are open seats in …" line, against the courses in published terms

  **Result:** no mismatches. The only items it flagged were expected ones: "Taking now" rows for Fall 2026, "No major course assigned" placeholders, and approved same-term ECO 461 pairings.
- **`check2.py`** checks every course title shown against `policy/courses/economics.json`. It checks every "Previously offered in …" line against `policy/historical-offerings/economics-advice.json`, and the summary box's Completed and Taking now lists against `students.json`. **Result:** no mismatches.
- **`conflicts.py`** checks that every PDF section letter exists in `policy/schedules/2026-2027.json` and that no two sections in one term overlap. **Result:** no conflicts.

### H2. "More information needed" and an overload prompt shown when graduating next year is proven impossible (students 09, 11)

**Exact lines**
- Both students: "Graduate next year:More information needed". The missing space after the colon is also wrong. (`09.txt:26`, `11.txt:26`)
- Student 09: "A plan may need more than the normal course limit in Winter 2027. You need advisor approval and must not be on academic probation." (`09.txt:31`)

**The same snapshots also say**

- The summary shows "Junior (graduating one year early)" next to "Graduation target: May 2028" (`09.txt:7-9`, `11.txt:7-9`).

**Why the data proves it impossible**
- Both students still need ECO 213. Fall 2026 is closed: initial drop/add ended 2026-08-28 (`policy/calendars/hanover-undergraduate.json:55`; `PLANNING-CURRENT-TERM-ADD-DROP`, `rules/terms-load-graduation.json:1516`). This assumes the November 22, 2026 plan date.
- Winter 2027 Economics coverage is COMPLETE and has no ECO 213 section (`policy/schedules/2026-2027.json:449-861`, coverage at `:862-869`).
- ECO 213 is banned in May (`PLANNING-MAY-COURSE-EXCLUSIONS`, `rules/terms-load-graduation.json:1086`).
- Course count also rules it out:
  - Student 09 has 8 required courses left. Student 11 has 10.
  - The normal limit is 4 courses in Winter plus 1 in May (`CAPACITY-WINTER` `:180`, `CAPACITY-MAY` `:278`). Even with an approved overload, Winter rises only to 5.25 units, so at most 5 + 1 = 6 courses.

**Owner rules it breaks**
- `policy/provenance/wording-review-decisions-2026-11-22.md:30`: "Before asking for overload approval, run the search with the approved ceiling. If it finds no plan, show the actual no-plan reason."
- OD4 (same file, `:19-21`): show the "courses left and terms" explanation when the remaining courses exceed capacity.

**Correct statement** (matching what students 10 and 12 already get at `10.txt:26` and `12.txt:26-27`): "You cannot finish by May 2027. ECO 213 is not offered in Winter or Spring/May 2027. The plan below is for May 2028." Drop the overload prompt. Make the standing label and the target agree.

### H3. Test-panel defect: study abroad was never applied (students 20, 21). Root cause is the test runner, not the app.

**What is wrong**
- Students 20 and 21 entered study abroad for Winter 2027, and student 20 also entered the following May (`tests/panel/students.json`, entries 20 and 21).
- Their plans still put ECO 221, ECO 321 and MAT 217 in Winter 2027 and ECO 222 in May 2027 (`plans/20.json`, `plans/21.json`).
- `snapshots/20.txt` and `snapshots/21.txt` are byte-identical to `snapshots/15.txt` (a student with no abroad term) apart from the student ID.
- OD4 says "A study-abroad term has zero capacity" (`wording-review-decisions-2026-11-22.md:21`).

**Root cause (evidence)**
- `scripts/panel/run.mjs:91` passes `studyAbroadTerm: "2027-WINTER"`.
- The app only recognises slot IDs like `winter-2027`: `lib/planning/study-abroad.js:15-17` returns no abroad terms when no slot matches, and `lib/planning/mcp-tools.js:88` validates the same `^(fall|winter|spring)-\d{4}$` format.
- The runner converts the overload term format at `run.mjs:107` but not the abroad term.
- It also never calls `updateStudyAbroadReservation` (`study-abroad.js:26-43`), which is what blocks the term's capacity.

**Effect:** the panel cannot certify any study-abroad wording. Production behaviour with a valid abroad term was not tested here.

**Remedy:** in `run.mjs`, convert the abroad term to `winter-2027` and apply `updateStudyAbroadReservation`. Then regenerate snapshots 20 and 21 and re-review them. `AGENTS.md:46` requires explicit owner authorisation before editing `scripts/panel/`.
