More about this plan
Other graduation requirements
HS ECO 113, ECO 114, and ECO 161 can each count toward Historical and Social Perspectives (HS), but only one of them can count. Your other HS course must be from a different discipline.
ECO 461 also counts toward your Writing 2 (W2) requirement.
