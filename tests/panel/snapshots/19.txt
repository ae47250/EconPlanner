More about this plan
Other graduation requirements
HS ECO 113 and ECO 114 can each count toward Historical and Social Perspectives (HS), but only one of them can count. Your other HS course must be from a different discipline.
ECO 461 also counts toward your Writing 2 (W2) requirement.

Winter 2027:

— Section J: Tue/Thu, 10:00–11:45 a.m.; Section K: Mon/Wed/Fri, 8:00–9:10 a.m.

— Tue/Thu, 8:00–9:45 a.m.

— Tue/Thu, 12:20–2:05 p.m.

— Tue/Thu, 10:00–11:45 a.m.
ECO 221 · Environmental Economics
— Mon/Wed/Fri, 9:20–10:30 a.m.
ECO 321 · Money and Financial Markets
— Tue/Thu, 12:20–2:05 p.m.
ECO 461 · Senior Seminar: Econometrics
— Mon/Wed/Fri, 10:40–11:50 a.m.
May 2027:
ECO 222 · Economics of the European Union
— Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri, 10:15 a.m.–12:30 p.m.
ECO 224 · Introduction to Sustainability
— Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri, 8:00–10:15 a.m.
ECO 234 · Rational Choices
— Mon/Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri, 10:15 a.m.–12:30 p.m.
Courses in future years
