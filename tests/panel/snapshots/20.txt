Other graduation requirements
HS ECO 113 and ECO 114 can each count toward Historical and Social Perspectives (HS), but only one of them can count. Your other HS course must be from a different discipline.
W2 ECO 461 also counts toward your Writing 2 (W2) requirement.

Past Economics course offerings
In past schedules, ECO 213 and ECO 214 were offered in different Fall or Winter terms. Their order changed from year to year. A published schedule may differ.
Past Fall schedules included: ECO 333, ECO 357.
Past Winter schedules included: ECO 221, ECO 337, ECO 357.

A past schedule does not guarantee a course will be offered again.
