# Economics Major Requirements and Prerequisites

Use this file for economics major structure, requirement groups, course facts, prerequisite rules, and title-to-code mappings.

This file is for planning support. It is not a replacement for the official college catalog or advisor confirmation.

## Major Requirements

The economics major requires:

- 11 major courses
- 1 cognate course
- Total: 12 courses

Required economics courses:

- `ECO 113` — Principles of Microeconomics
- `ECO 114` — Principles of Macroeconomics
- `ECO 213` — Intermediate Microeconomics
- `ECO 214` — Intermediate Macroeconomics
- `ECO 257` — Quantitative Methods for Business/Economics, or `MAT 217`, or `PSY 217`
- `ECO 321` — Money & Financial Markets, or `BUSN 323`
- `ECO 461` — Senior Seminar: Econometrics

Cognate requirement:

- `MAT 113` or `MAT 121`

Elective requirement:

- Four additional ECO courses
- Only one of the four additional ECO electives may be at the 100 level
- The cognate course does not count as one of the 11 major courses
- A non-ECO substitute does not count as an ECO elective unless advisor confirmation is provided

## Elective Counting Rule

Any ECO course may count as an economics elective except required ECO courses:

- `ECO 113`
- `ECO 114`
- `ECO 213`
- `ECO 214`
- `ECO 257`
- `ECO 321`
- `ECO 461`

`BUSN 323`, `MAT 217`, and `PSY 217` do not count as ECO electives.

If `MAT 217` or `PSY 217` satisfies the statistics option, do not count `ECO 257` as an elective by default. Flag advisor confirmation if the student wants to use `ECO 257` that way.

## Required Course Sequence Logic

1. `ECO 113` and `ECO 114` are the normal starting economics courses.
2. `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` is needed before `ECO 213` and `ECO 214`.
3. `ECO 213` requires `ECO 113` plus `MAT 113` or `MAT 121`.
4. `ECO 214` requires `ECO 114` plus `MAT 113` or `MAT 121`.
5. `ECO 257` requires `ECO 113` or `ECO 114`.
6. `ECO 321` requires `ECO 113` and `ECO 114`.
7. `ECO 461` requires `ECO 213`, `ECO 214`, and `ECO 257`, `MAT 217`, or `PSY 217`.
8. `ECO 471` requires `ECO 213`, `ECO 214`, and `ECO 321`.

## Timing Rules

- `ECO 461` requires senior standing and may only be scheduled in senior Winter.
- The statistics option must be completed no later than Fall of senior year because it is needed before Winter senior-year `ECO 461`.
- It is recommended to complete the statistics option by the end of junior year.
- It is recommended, when feasible, to complete both `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` by the end of junior year.
- Do not state that `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` must be completed by the end of junior year.

## Advisor-Confirmation Flags

- If a student uses `BUSN 323` instead of `ECO 321`, confirm whether it satisfies any later course prerequisite that specifically names `ECO 321`.
- `ECO 357` requires department permission.
- Directed study and special topics courses may require advisor or department approval depending on context.
- Transfer credit, AP credit, substitutions, waivers, and older catalog-year cases require advisor confirmation.
