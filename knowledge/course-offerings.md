# Economics Course Offerings Rules

Use this file for general offering patterns. Use `knowledge/course-schedule-2026-2027.md` for known 2026/2027 offerings. The current schedule file overrides general patterns for that academic year.

These are planning rules, not a replacement for checking the official schedule.

## Fall and Winter

- `ECO 113` and `ECO 114` are offered in both Fall and Winter.
- `ECO 113` and `ECO 114` usually have many sections each term.
- `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` are Fall/Winter courses only.
- `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` are never offered in the same term.
- `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` are each offered once per academic year, but without a future schedule the exact term is unknown.
- For future unscheduled years, never display only `ECO 213` or only `ECO 214`. Use `ECO 213 or ECO 214` and later `ECO 214 or ECO 213` when the exact term is unknown.
- Below any year using those alternatives, add a note: `ECO 213 and ECO 214 are offered only in Fall or Winter, once per year each, so the exact future term is not known yet.`
- Do not place `ECO 213` or `ECO 214` in May Term.
- Schedule `ECO 213` and `ECO 214` as soon as feasible after `MAT 113` or `MAT 121` and the appropriate introductory courses are complete.

## May Term

- May Term usually offers economics electives.
- May Term usually has one or two 100-level or 200-level economics electives.
- Students should take only one May Term course.
- Do not schedule more than one May Term course for any student.
- Do not schedule more than one ECO elective in May Term.
- For incoming first-year students, do not schedule a May Term ECO course in the first year.
- If known May options exist for the first year, list them as optional notes below the first-year block rather than scheduling them.
- When using May Term for economics in years without a known schedule, write `ECO elective` unless the student provides current course-offering information.

Never place these courses in May Term:

- `ECO 113`
- `ECO 114`
- `ECO 213`
- `ECO 214`
- `ECO 321`
- `BUSN 323`
- `MAT 113`
- `MAT 121`
- `MAT 217`
- `ECO 257`
- `PSY 217`
- `ECO 461`

## Senior Seminar / Capstone

- `ECO 461` is the senior seminar / capstone.
- `ECO 461` can only be taken in senior year.
- `ECO 461` is offered in Winter term only.
- Never place `ECO 461` in Fall or May Term.
- Never place `ECO 461` before senior year.

## Requirement-Substitution Notes

### `MAT 217 / ECO 257 / PSY 217`

- The statistics requirement can be satisfied by `MAT 217`, `ECO 257`, or `PSY 217`.
- Because the department has not been able to offer `ECO 257` reliably, do not suggest `ECO 257` by itself.
- When suggesting this requirement, write it as: `MAT 217 / ECO 257 / PSY 217`.
- Do not place `MAT 217`, `ECO 257`, or `PSY 217` in May Term.
- The statistics option must be completed no later than Fall of senior year because it is needed before Winter senior-year `ECO 461`.
- It is recommended to complete the statistics option by the end of junior year.
- For `ECO 461` planning, `ECO 257`, `MAT 217`, or `PSY 217` satisfies the statistics prerequisite pathway.

### `ECO 321 / BUSN 323`

- `BUSN 323` can be taken instead of `ECO 321`.
- When suggesting this requirement, write it as: `ECO 321 / BUSN 323`.
- `BUSN 323` is usually offered in Fall/Winter only for planning purposes.
- `ECO 321` is offered in Winter in the known 2026/2027 schedule.
- Do not place `ECO 321` or `BUSN 323` in May Term.
- Do not schedule or mention `ECO 471` in standard plans. Include `ECO 471` only if the student explicitly asks for Senior Thesis.
- `ECO 471` requires senior standing and may only be scheduled in senior year.

## Elective Rules Relevant to Offerings

- Four additional ECO electives are required for the economics major.
- Only one economics elective can be at the 100 level.
- Use known schedule data to name specific ECO electives for 2026/2027 when prerequisites allow.
- For any academic year without a specific schedule file, write `ECO elective` instead of naming a specific ECO elective.
- Use only official ECO courses listed in the major requirements file if naming a specific elective.
- Check prerequisites before naming any specific elective.
- Do not schedule more than one ECO elective in any term unless the student explicitly asks for a heavier economics load or it is needed to finish the major on time.
- For senior year, plan no more than one ECO elective unless absolutely necessary for major completion.
- Labor Economics must be treated as `ECO 331` and requires `ECO 213` first.
