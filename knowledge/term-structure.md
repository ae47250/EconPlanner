# Term Structure

This college uses three academic terms each year: Fall, Winter, and May.

Summer is not part of the regular Fall/Winter/May planning sequence. No Summer Economics offering is verified by the current repository schedule evidence.

## Fall Term

- Approximate timing: late August to early December.
- Length: 13 weeks.
- Normal student load: 4 courses.
- This planner should not schedule more than 2 economics courses in Fall.

## Winter Term

- Approximate timing: early January to mid-April.
- Length: 13 weeks.
- Normal student load: 4 courses.
- This planner should not schedule more than 2 economics courses in Winter.

## May Term

- Approximate timing: May.
- Length: 4 weeks.
- Normal student load: 1 course.
- May Term is a short intensive term.
- This planner should not schedule more than 1 total May Term course.
- This planner should not schedule more than 1 ECO course in May.

## Date and Academic-Year Logic

- Use the college academic cycle, not the student browser location, for term logic.
- Use Hanover's official academic calendar at `https://www.hanover.edu/academics/calendar/` for exact boundaries when captured.
- For Summer 2026, clarification is required from May 23 through August 23. Fall 2026 classes begin August 24.
- Fall/Winter/May belong to one academic year, labeled like `2026/2027`.
- If current date is in summer, begin with the upcoming Fall.
- If current date is in Fall and the student is still choosing or changing Fall courses, include Fall; otherwise begin with Winter.
- If current date is in Winter and the student is still choosing or changing Winter courses, include Winter; otherwise begin with May if relevant.
- If current date is in May and the student is still choosing or changing May courses, include May; otherwise begin with the upcoming Fall.
- For version 1, if no exact current-term context is supplied, begin with Fall 2026 and the `2026/2027` academic year.

## Course-Load Reference

- Fall: normally 4 total courses.
- Winter: normally 4 total courses.
- May: normally 1 total course.

The app displays only economics major courses and directly related math/stat/business courses. It does not fill non-major general-education or elective slots.
