## May 2027 Note

May Term is listed as Spring in the source schedule. Section code `S` means Spring/May Term.

For an incoming first-year student, do not schedule a May 2027 ECO course in the table. Below the first-year block, note:

`Known May 2027 options: ECO 222 or ECO 260. ECO 222 requires ECO 114. ECO 260 topic varies. Since May is optional, you would take one of these only if it fits your plan.`
